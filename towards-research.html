Research

  • Worked as a Junior Research Fellow (JRF) on a DRDO-funded project “Analysis of NIST’s Lattice based PQC schemes for Digital Signature” under the guidance of Dr. Sumit Kumar Pandey and Dr. Sartaj Ul Hasan at IIT Jammu from Sep 2024 to May 2025.

Projects

  • Reinforcement Learning for Quantum Error Correction
    Benchmarked DQN and supervised MLP decoders against PyMatching (MWPM) on rotated surface codes under circuit-level Pauli noise.
  • Physics-Informed Reinforcement Learning for Noisy Quantum Circuit Optimization
    PPO-based circuit synthesis using a physics-informed reward combining fidelity shaping, Lindbladian decoherence (T1/T2), and gate error penalties.
  • Quantum-on-Quantum RL for Noise-Aware Circuit Synthesis
    Hybrid quantum-classical RL using PennyLane parameterized quantum circuits to compile low-depth circuits under depolarizing noise.
  • Quantum MNIST Classification
    Controlled comparison of a hybrid variational quantum classifier against classical MLP and cosine-feature baselines on MNIST.
